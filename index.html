HOW TO SURVIVE

01

THE ECHO LOOP

Your movement is recorded continuously. Every 8 seconds, an echo of your previous 8 seconds spawns and replays it exactly. Up to several echoes can be alive at once.

8s RECORDING → GHOST REPLAY

ECHOES CAN: collect orbs, charge relay switches, destroy enemies. Enemies are attracted to them. They are your decoys, weapons and keys.
